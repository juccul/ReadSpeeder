# ReadSpeeder privacy policy

Last updated: August 14, 2026

ReadSpeeder does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data with the
developer or third parties. The production app does not request network access
and does not contain advertising, analytics, or user accounts.

## Data stored by the app

ReadSpeeder processes imported TXT, PDF, and EPUB documents and pasted text on
the device. It stores document content, extracted covers and metadata, reading
progress, and app settings in the app's private storage so the library can be
restored between sessions.

## Android backup

Android may include ReadSpeeder's private app data in the user's system backup,
depending on the device, Android version, and backup settings. Those backups are
controlled by the device user and the operating-system backup provider; the
ReadSpeeder developer does not receive them.

## Retention and deletion

Users can delete individual documents or clear the library from within the app.
Clearing app storage or uninstalling ReadSpeeder removes its on-device data.
Copies already held by an Android backup provider are governed by that
provider's retention controls.
